A Statewide Siting Tool for Coastal Enhanced Weathering on the California Coast

Authors Ella Coulson, Connor Mack

Coastal enhanced weathering spreads crushed silicate minerals on sandy beaches. Wave energy grinds them down, and the reaction draws CO2 out of the atmosphere and stores it in seawater as dissolved bicarbonate.

Whether it is suitable in California depends on several questions at once — chemical, ecological, regulatory, and logistical. This work takes on all four across the state's open coast, divided into 3,148 one-kilometre segments: the ocean conditions that set how fast the minerals dissolve, the habitats and protected areas that rule stretches of coast out entirely, the permits and environmental review a deployment would face, and how far each segment sits from the sand-moving operations and mineral sources that would supply it.

Suitability Map — the June 2026 capstone version, which ranks the eligible coast by a composite score built from ocean conditions.

Siting Tool — where the work went next: same segments, no score, and a different question — what it would take to work there, each threshold stated on its own.

Opens on YouTube. ↗ Capstone Suitability Map The June 2026 capstone map. It ranks: all 3,148 segments are drawn, and the 430 eligible ones carry a composite suitability score — the mean of four sub-scores for dissolution, residence, efficiency and storage. Every input is oceanographic: wave energy, temperature, pH, beach slope, longshore current, wind, mixed-layer depth, aragonite saturation. Regulation sets which segments are eligible to be scored, and marine protected areas and critical habitat appear as warnings on each segment — but neither moves the score. → Current Siting Tool The current tool, on the same 3,148 segments. It does not rank and has no composite score; it asks what it would take to work somewhere, in three steps. A five-gate screen for whether a segment is workable at all — beach substrate, open coast, protected habitat. Then the permits and environmental reviews each of the 430 survivors would trigger. Then two stated thresholds that narrow the eligible coast. Wave height and temperature are still on every segment’s card as context; here they decide nothing. →

The two maps use these layers differently, on purpose.
